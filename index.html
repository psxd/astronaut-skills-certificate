03

How points are awarded

both pillars required
01

Points equal credit hours

A 3-credit course is 3 points; the 1-credit seminar is 1. Academic total is 18.

02

One core, both tracks open

Complete the core of either track. Courses from the other track still count as electives.

03

Breadth inside the 18

At least two track electives and at least two interdisciplinary courses, not just one narrow column.

04

Workshops must span all four areas

One activity per area at minimum. A strong showing in one area never covers an empty one.

05

Each activity counts once

Repeats, retakes, and duplicated special-topics sections add nothing to the total.

06

No transferring between pillars

Surplus workshop points cannot cover a coursework shortfall, and the reverse. Both must be met.

07

Evidence required

Certifications need a valid card, workshops documented attendance, courses a passing grade.

08

Thirty points, no rounding

18 academic + 12 training = 30. Partial points are not awarded or carried over.

Course points follow catalog credit hours. Workshop points are weighted by contact hours and certification level, and the 18 / 12 split between pillars is the proposal underlying this page — adjust both freely without disturbing the layout.
